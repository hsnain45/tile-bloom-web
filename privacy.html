Tile Bloom — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

Tile Bloom is a relaxing offline puzzle game. We respect your privacy:

  • No data collection. Tile Bloom does not collect, store on our servers, sell, or share any personal data.
  • No accounts. The game has no sign-up or login.
  • No analytics or advertising trackers. The game contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • On-device progress only. Your game progress, garden, and settings are stored only on your own device and never leave it.
  • No permissions. The game does not request access to your contacts, photos, location, camera, or files.

If you contact us by email, we will only use your message to reply to you.
